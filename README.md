A Discrete Time Model of Delayed Allergic Skin Reactions to Intradermal Tattoos

Allergic skin reactions from a tattoo occur approximately 6-10\% of the time. These reactions can be delayed weeks, months, or even years post-application. Problematically, these reactions can be difficult to anticipate, and model is beneficial to analyze these dynamics and predict long-term results. Our model is a system of coupled difference equations with a compartmental structure representing the physical state of the skin, considering influential factors such as personal sensitivity, UV exposure, and long-term allergen accumulation.
